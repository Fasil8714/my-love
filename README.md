# 💍 Romantic Anniversary Gift Website

A super romantic, fully animated anniversary gift website handcrafted for your husband.

---

## ✨ Features Included
1. **Curtain Reveal Opening**:
   - Velvet curtains and glowing heart gate with the "Open My Gift" button.
   - Triggers heart explosion particles and starts the romantic ambient melody.
2. **Falling Rose Petals & Starlight Bokeh Canvas**:
   - Realistic 2D physics simulation of falling rose petals and twinkling stars drifting in the background.
   - Interactive screen clicks emit floating glowing hearts & sparkles.
3. **Live "Love in Every Heartbeat" Counter**:
   - Real-time live countdown/counter showing exact **Years, Months, Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds** with heartbeat pulsation.
   - Automatically calculates your milestone since your anniversary date.
4. **Our Beautiful Love Story (Timeline)**:
   - Interactive glowing timeline with chapters: First Meeting, First Date, Wedding Day, Everyday Adventures, and Today & Tomorrow.
5. **Polaroid Moments Gallery**:
   - 3D tilted Polaroid photos with pins, handwritten cursive captions, and a click-to-zoom Lightbox modal.
6. **"Reasons Why I Love You" (3D Flip Cards)**:
   - 6 interactive cards with 3D perspective flip animations revealing heartfelt love reasons.
7. **Wax-Sealed Love Letter**:
   - A 3D wax seal ("Sealed with Love"). Clicking the seal breaks the seal and slides open an authentic parchment letter.
8. **The Golden Love Lock Bridge**:
   - Digital golden padlock where you can write an anniversary wish together and click "Lock Forever" to trigger full-screen fireworks!
9. **Soothing Romantic Music Player**:
   - Built-in Web Audio API romantic melody synthesizer (plays gentle acoustic piano/chimes without needing external audio files).
   - Spinning vinyl record widget at the bottom left with play/pause toggle.
10. **Instant In-Browser Personalizer (Customize Button)**:
    - Click the floating **✨ Customize** button (bottom right) to update:
      - Husband's Name & Your Name
      - Anniversary Date (updates the live counter automatically!)
      - Banner milestone title & romantic quote
      - Personal letter message
    - Saves directly into `localStorage`!

## 🚀 How to Run & View
- **Option 1 (Easiest)**: Double click [index.html](file:///d:/MyProjects/PersanalProjects/Aniversary/index.html) to open directly in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
- **Option 2 (Local Server)**: Run `node server.js` in terminal and visit `http://localhost:8088/`.

## 📷 How to Add Your Real Photos
In the folder `assets/images/`, simply replace:
- `hero.jpg`: Main big banner photo (fairy lights / couple portrait)
- `beach.jpg`: Memory photo 1 (walk / travel)
- `dinner.jpg`: Memory photo 2 (dinner / celebration)
- `sparkler.jpg`: Memory photo 3 (smiles / special moment)
